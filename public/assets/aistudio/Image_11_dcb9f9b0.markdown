## Brand & Style
The design system evokes the mysterious, windswept shores of the *Pontus Euxinus* (the Black Sea)—an ancient realm where Hellenic colonies, Persian satrapies, and maritime lore converged. Designed specifically for an immersive dark-mode mobile roleplay experience, the aesthetic pairs classical monumental antiquity with tactile craftsmanship: chiselled obsidian tablets, worn bronze drachmas, wax-sealed decrees, and lapidary inscriptions.

The design movement mixes **Tactile Antiquity** with **Refined Editorial Minimalism**. It avoids glossy modern glassmorphism in favor of atmospheric chthonic depth: matte stone finishes, hairline gold-leaf filigree, meander-inspired geometric framing, and parchment-ink contrast. The interface feels ancient yet razor-sharp—an artifact unearthed from a coastal necropolis, functioning as an interactive codex of laws, inventories, and diplomatic oaths.

## Colors
The palette captures the nocturnal gloom of the Euxine deeps pierced by votive flames and mint-struck coin:

- **Foundation (Obsidian & Slate):**
  - Base Abyss: `#0B0F14` (canvas surface, depths of the sea)
  - Basalt Layer 1: `#121820` (tablets, modal panels, bottom sheets)
  - Basalt Layer 2: `#1B232E` (card surfaces, interactive elements, slots)
  - Basalt Highlight: `#263140` (card headers, dividers, pressed states)

- **Primary (Pontic Gold & Bronze):**
  - Sunlit Stater: `#E5C158` (active states, primary highlights, critical emphasis)
  - Antique Gold: `#D4AF37` (primary brand, prominent CTA surfaces, seals)
  - Patina Bronze: `#9A7B38` (borders, subtle icons, inactive tab highlights)
  - Burnished Ore: `#5E481E` (subtle border tracks, inactive frames)

- **Text & Parchment Scale:**
  - Aged Parchment: `#F3E5AB` (primary headline & critical readout text)
  - Chiseled Stone: `#E2DAC2` (primary body text, crisp legibility)
  - Ochre Dust: `#C2B280` (secondary body, metadata, inventory annotations)
  - Weathered Stele: `#7C786A` (placeholder, timestamps, disabled indicators)

- **Accents & Heraldry:**
  - Terracotta & Blood Crimson: `#8B2635` / `#C0392B` (danger, sacrifice, martial badges, wax seals)
  - Euxine Patina & Sacred Olive: `#2E5A44` / `#4E8765` (boons, treaties, prosperity, affirmative states)

## Typography
Typography reflects the duel between epigraphic monumentality and utilitarian administrative clarity. 

1. **Titles & Inscriptions (Playfair Display):** Imparts the dramatic, classical transitional elegance reminiscent of stone dedications, dramatic oracles, and historic chronicles.
2. **Heraldry, Badges & Labels (Cinzel):** Employs classical Roman-Hellenic proportions inspired by first-century epigraphy. Rendered with small capitals and tracked letter spacing for coin inscriptions, roleplay titles, stat counters, and decree headers.
3. **Manuscripts & Inventory Data (Source Sans 3):** Provides humanist clarity and supreme high-density legibility on small OLED screens. Utilized for dialogue trees, item stats, character sheets, and gameplay logs to prevent reading fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing
Designed from a mobile-first paradigm optimized for handheld inventory management, decree drafting, and episodic roleplay encounters:

- **Grid Architecture:** 4-column layout on mobile devices (`<600px`) expanding to an 8-column layout on foldable or tablet displays (`600px - 1024px`). Outer boundary margins adhere strictly to `1rem` (`16px`) to maximize horizontal screen real estate for two-column inventory grids and stat matrices.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Spacing operates strictly on a 4px/8px modular cadence. Content units within stone tablets and parchment scrolls use `space-md` (`12px`) for interior breathing room and `space-lg` (`20px`) between discrete thematic blocks (e.g., Quest Objectives vs. Rewards).
- **Tactile Density:** Layout emphasizes high-information density suitable for antique RPG sheets, using tight element gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) balanced by generous sectional dividers.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is not communicated through generic, neutral drop shadows, but through **stratified chthonic layering and incandescent halos**:

- **Basalt Stacking (Elevation via Tone):** Elevated interfaces emerge from the Obsidian floor (`#0B0F14`). Floating panels, bottom sheets, and dialogue prompts sit at `#121820`, while selectable inventory tiles and cards rest at `#1B232E`.
- **Chiseled Borders:** Surfaces define boundary edges using a crisp 1px hairline border: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` for resting states, shifting to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.65)` when highlighted or focused.
- **Votive Gold Glow:** Elevated interactive objects (such as Drachma confirmation buttons or rare relics) produce an inner specular ridge (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(229, 193, 88, 0.4)`) paired with an amber ambient halo (`0 4px 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
- **Wax Impressions:** Debossed elements (sockets, unequipped slots, text fields) utilize an inner shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`) framed by a burnished perimeter (`#5E481E`).

## Shapes
The shape system embodies **chiseled masonry**. Heavy pill-shapes and hyper-rounded bubbly containers are strictly avoided; elements are shaped with soft, masonry-carved bevels (`0.25rem` / `4px` base radius):

- **Parchments & Tablets (`roundedness: 1`):** Subtle `4px` corner beveling simulates hand-cut stone stele and smoothed slate.
- **Notched & Meander Corner Motifs:** Prominent hero cards and dialogue modals feature decorative 45-degree corner bevels or geometric stepped corners echoing ancient Hellenic architectural frets.
- **Seals & Coins:** Medallions, coin counters, and avatar seals retain pure circular geometry (`rounded-full`), counterbalancing the rigid planar stone grids.

## Components

### Buttons & Oaths (CTAs)
- **Primary (The Golden Stater):** Rich metallic background gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, #E5C158 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #9A7B38 100%)`) with obsidian text (`#0B0F14`, font: `Cinzel`, weight: `700`, uppercase). Edged with a 1px inner rim (`#F3E5AB`).
- **Secondary (Basalt Stele):** Surface in `#1B232E`, edged with a 1px border in Antique Bronze (`#9A7B38`). Text in Aged Parchment (`#F3E5AB`).
- **Sacrificial / Destructive:** Muted Terracotta Crimson surface (`#8B2635`) with blood-red rim (`#C0392B`) and parchment labeling.

### Stele Cards & Inventory Grids
- **Container Structure:** Rendered in `#121820` with a 1px `#263140` structural border. Header rows are crowned with a subtle meander-inspired double hairline divider in Patina Bronze.
- **Inventory Sockets:** Square aspect-ratio tiles (`#0B0F14`) featuring an inset shadow. Active or equipped items glow with an antique gold frame; rarity tiers transition from Iron (`#7C786A`), Bronze (`#9A7B38`), Gold (`#D4AF37`), to Sacred Olive (`#4E8765`).

### Seals, Chips & Stat Badges
- **Status Tags:** Compact, uppercase `Cinzel` labels wrapped in a 1px border.
  - *Tribute / Boon:* Background `rgba(46, 90, 68, 0.25)`, border `#4E8765`, text `#A3D9B8`.
  - *Omen / Threat:* Background `rgba(139, 38, 53, 0.25)`, border `#C0392B`, text `#F1A9B2`.
  - *Status / Rank:* Background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`, border `#D4AF37`, text `#F3E5AB`.
- **Wax Seals:** Circular badges pinned to top-right card corners bearing engraved Hellenic sigils in embossed Terracotta (`#8B2635`).

### Inputs & Scroll Inscriptions
- **Field Canvas:** Dark recess (`#0B0F14`) with a 1px boundary in `#5E481E`. 
- **Focus State:** Border sharpens to `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
- **Typography:** Input text displays in crisp `Source Sans 3` (`#E2DAC2`), accompanied by floating epigraphic field labels in `Cinzel` (`#C2B280`).

### Checkboxes, Radios & Oracles
- **Checkboxes:** Square stone sockets. Checked state renders a gold Greek cross or meander motif in `#E5C158`.
- **Radios:** Concentric bronze coin bezels inset with an obsidian core that fills with a glowing amber dot upon selection.

### Custom Component: The Meander Divider
A horizontal layout separator featuring a centered ornamental Greek key glyph framed by hairline gold-leaf gradients tapering to transparent obsidian at screen edges.
